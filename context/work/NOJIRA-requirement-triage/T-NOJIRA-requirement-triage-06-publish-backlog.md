# Publish the triaged, ordered backlog of eight

> From [P-NOJIRA-requirement-triage](../../plan/NOJIRA-requirement-triage/plan.md).

## Objective
The deliverable the whole plan exists for. Every one of the thirty-one has exactly one disposition,
each disposition is backed by evidence or a named owner, and the remaining eight are in an argued
order.

The measure is what the next owner does on their first day: read it and start, or spend two weeks
reconstructing it.

## Steps
- [ ] Assemble the dispositions from the preceding tasks and check that all thirty-one are accounted for exactly once — nothing listed twice, nothing missing
- [ ] Order the eight remaining, with a one-line rationale per position rather than a bare sequence
- [ ] State which parts of the order are provisional on the baseline and which are not, so a reader knows what is settled
- [ ] Put R25 near the front and say why: it is the one of the eight the prior attempt identified as producing visibly wrong output
- [ ] Keep the deferred, retired and measured items visible in the document rather than only the eight — a backlog that shows only the work hides why the rest is absent
- [ ] Write it so it reads without the analyses behind it, and link to them for anyone who wants the reasoning

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] All thirty-one appear exactly once, each with a disposition
- [ ] The eight are ordered with a rationale per position and a statement of what is provisional
- [ ] The deferred, measured and handed-off items remain visible alongside the eight
- [ ] A reader who has not followed this work can start from it without reconstructing the reasoning

## Verification
Give it to someone who has not been involved and ask them what they would pick up first and why. If
they have to ask a question the document should have answered, it is not finished.
